title=[B42.14+] Profession Keys
description=Do you find it weird that some professions do not have keys to their profession building?

description=I created this mod because while playing Week One, I kept thinking that a police officer

description=before the apocalypse should realistically have access to the police station and its armory.

description=

description=After doing some work on it for my own playthrough, I decided to clean it up and share it

description=with the community.

description=

description=This mod gives profession-based access to service buildings in a simple and safe way.

description=

description=

description=CURRENT FEATURES

description==================

description=

description=• Police officers can gain access to a police station

description=• Firefighters can gain access to a fire station

description=

description=When you enter a valid building for your profession for the first time, the game gives

description=your character the correct building key.

description=

description=A short message is shown to indicate that the key was found.

description=

description=Only one building is ever assigned.

description=The first valid station you enter is the one you keep for the rest of the save.

description=

description=

description=RULES AND LIMITATIONS

description======================

description=

description=• Only one building per profession

description=• No duplicate keys

description=• Other stations stay locked

description=• Uses the correct building key ID (same as debug-spawned keys)

description=• Fully persistent across save/load

description=

description=The mod does not give free access at spawn and does not unlock doors by script.

description=It simply gives the proper key when it makes sense.

description=

description=

description=HOW BUILDINGS ARE DETECTED

description===========================

description=

description=• Buildings are detected by their internal room layout

description=• No hardcoded map names

description=• No coordinates or zones

description=• Works on vanilla and modded maps (as long as rooms are named properly)

description=

description=

description=SUPPORTED PROFESSIONS

description======================

description=

description=• Police Officer

description=• Fire Officer

description=

description=More professions may be added later.

description=

description=

description=COMPATIBILITY AND TESTING

description==========================

description=

description=• Tested on Build 42.12 and Build 42.13

description=• Can be added to an existing save

description=• Compatible with every mod I tested (I play with 100+ active mods)

description=• I did not test it on multiplayer. Once I do, I will update the mod or leave information here that it works.

description=

description=The mod does not modify vanilla data or override existing systems, which makes it safe

description=to use alongside other mods.

description=

description=
